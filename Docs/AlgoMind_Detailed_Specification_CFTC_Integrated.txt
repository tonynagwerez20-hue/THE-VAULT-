1. System Purpose and Scope
AlgoMind is a modular XAUUSD research, decision and execution system intended to reproduce an institutional-style workflow using data that can realistically be assembled in a retail environment. It combines market structure, liquidity, value, retail order-flow proxies, volatility, sessions, news, positioning context and strict execution/risk controls.
Python is responsible for research, feature engineering, regime intelligence and strategy scoring.
MQL5 is the final execution and hard-risk authority.
The deterministic rule-based engine is the reference implementation; ML is optional.
Backtest, replay, forward and live modes use the same versioned feature and decision contracts.
Retail flow is explicitly treated as a proxy rather than centralized institutional order flow.
CFTC is explicitly treated as slow positioning context rather than intraday order flow.
Missing, stale or malformed critical data causes a safe veto rather than uncontrolled trading.
2. Design Principles
Causality: every historical decision uses only information available at that timestamp.
Modularity: data, features, regime, strategy, risk and execution are independently testable.
Auditability: every decision can be reconstructed from timestamped inputs and versioned configuration.
Broker neutrality: symbol precision, contract size, tick value and minimum volume are read from the broker.
Fail closed: system uncertainty blocks new entries.
Separation of evidence: CFTC, retail flow, news and hypothesis-based positioning sources retain separate provenance.

5. MQL5 Execution Layer
Receive or read TradeIntent messages.
Revalidate current market price, spread and broker symbol specifications.
Calculate volume using actual tick value, stop distance, account equity and broker constraints.
Apply session, spread, news, margin and loss-limit gates.

Manage stops, partial exits and trailing logic.
Record execution lifecycle and broker response.
Broker normalization is mandatory because gold symbols can have different digits, point sizes, contract specifications and minimum trade sizes.

11. CFTC Commitments of Traders Integration
CFTC COT is now a formal AlgoMind external-data layer. The CFTC publishes weekly positioning reports covering report families including Legacy, Disaggregated and Traders in Financial Futures (TFF). The Disaggregated reports classify reportable traders such as Producer/Merchant/Processor/User, Swap Dealers, Managed Money and Other Reportables; TFF uses financial-market categories such as Dealer, Asset Manager, Leveraged Money and Other Reportables. citeturn0search2turn0search4
For gold, AlgoMind should initially use the relevant CFTC gold futures market from the appropriate report family and explicitly configure its relationship to the broker's XAUUSD spot/CFD symbol. Futures positioning is not assumed to equal spot positioning.

11.2 CFTC timing and point-in-time control
CFTC states that weekly Futures-Only and Futures-and-Options-Combined COT reports are released Friday at 3:30 p.m. Eastern Time. AlgoMind therefore stores both the report/as-of date and a publication-availability timestamp. citeturn0search7
Critical rule: a historical strategy decision may use a CFTC observation only after that observation was publicly available. Joining by report date alone is prohibited.

11.5 CFTC-to-XAUUSD Mapping
The CFTC adapter must explicitly record the selected futures contract, report family, contract continuity/roll methodology, symbol mapping and version. The active CFTC state is carried forward between eligible releases; no artificial intraday CFTC value is generated.
The mapping must be tested because XAUUSD is normally a broker-specific spot/CFD instrument while CFTC data represents regulated futures positioning.

28. Research Hypotheses and Known Limitations
28.1 Limitations
Retail MT5 does not provide complete institutional/global order-book information.
Broker tick volume is not centralized exchange volume.
Retail execution introduces spread, slippage and latency.
Minimum lot sizes can distort small-account risk targeting.
CFTC is weekly and slow relative to intraday gold price action.
CFTC futures positioning is not identical to spot XAUUSD positioning.
Historical performance depends on data quality and broker-specific assumptions.
28.2 Hypotheses to test
Retail order-flow proxies add information beyond structure.
Delta divergence/transition improves timing.
VWAP/value improves selection.
Regime-aware strategy switching improves robustness.
CFTC positioning extremes or changes add contextual value to XAUUSD.
Combining slow CFTC context with fast retail flow improves conditional signal quality.
ML gatekeeping improves trade quality without replacing deterministic controls.

30. Engineering Summary
The completed AlgoMind architecture is a layered decision system: fast MT5 price/structure/liquidity/retail-flow state, medium-horizon value and regime intelligence, event-based news context, and slow external positioning context from CFTC. These layers feed a deterministic strategy engine and are ultimately constrained by MQL5 risk and execution controls.
The CFTC layer strengthens contextual positioning analysis but does not replace fast market information. Its most important engineering requirement is point-in-time correctness: the strategy must use the CFTC observation only after its actual public availability.
